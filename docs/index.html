Federal actions and congressional trades

TheGovWatcher is a nonpartisan reader for two kinds of public records: what the federal government does, and stock trades members of Congress report under the STOCK Act. It links to official sources. It does not rate, endorse, or oppose any party, member, or policy.

Feeds

  • 01 Federal government actions bills, votes, executive orders, and Federal Register items.
  • 02 Congressional trades periodic transaction reports, with links to the filings.
  • 03 Scraper status last run, records collected, and errors.

Records are loaded from official sources. See About for what is collected and what is only linked.

Official public records only. Not affiliated with the U.S. government.
