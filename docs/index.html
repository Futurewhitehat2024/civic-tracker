Federal actions and congressional trades

is a nonpartisan reader for two kinds of public records: what the federal government does, and stock trades members of Congress report under the STOCK Act. It links to official sources. It does not rate, endorse, or oppose any party, member, or policy.

Feeds

  • Federal government actions — bills, votes, executive orders, and Federal Register items.
  • Congressional trades — periodic transaction reports, with links to the filings.
  • Scraper status — last run, records collected, and errors.

Federal actions are loaded from official records. Congressional trade filings are not collected yet. See About for sources and limits.

Official public records only. Not affiliated with the U.S. government.
