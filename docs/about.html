About this site

A reading room for the public record.

TheGovWatcher publishes public records and points to the original documents. It does not add a score, a recommendation, or a verdict.

Purpose Sources What you will find Update schedule How to read a disclosure Disclaimer

Purpose

TheGovWatcher is a nonpartisan place to read two kinds of public records: what the federal government does, and the stock trades members of Congress disclose. It does not support or oppose candidates, parties, or legislation.

Party labels are shown only when an official source includes them, and they are shown as reported.

Sources

Information comes from official public records: the Federal Register, Congress.gov, the House Clerk, Senate.gov, and congressional financial disclosure filings.

Campaign websites, social media, and news articles are not sources for the records on this site.

What you will find

Federal actions include bills, floor votes, executive orders, and Federal Register documents. A row shows the title, date, identifier, chamber when there is one, and the source name. The link opens the official page.

Congressional trades are periodic transaction reports filed under the STOCK Act. A House row shows the member, the transaction, the asset, the ticker when the filing gives one, the amount range, the owner when the filing names one, and the dates, with a link to the official document. When a House filing is a scanned document, the row links to that document.

A Senate row links to the Senate’s official financial disclosure search, where that senator’s periodic transaction reports are published. The search page states its own terms before it shows filings.

Update schedule

Records on this site are updated daily. The date on each page is the date of the records shown there.

How to read a disclosure

A periodic transaction report is a disclosure. It is not evidence of improper conduct. Amounts are ranges as filed, not exact dollar figures. The owner may be the member, a spouse, a dependent, or a joint owner, when the filing says so.

A missing row does not mean a record does not exist. The official filing or government page is the record.

Disclaimer

TheGovWatcher is not affiliated with the U.S. government. It is not legal, financial, or voting advice. Read the linked official document before you rely on a row.
