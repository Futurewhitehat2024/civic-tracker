About this site

About

Nonpartisan

TheGovWatcher does not support or oppose candidates, parties, or legislation. It publishes public records and points at the original documents. Party labels, when a filing or official source includes them, are shown as reported.

Official sources only

Records are meant to come only from official publications. Planned sources are Congress.gov, House and Senate periodic transaction reports filed under the STOCK Act, the Federal Register, and executive-order publications on whitehouse.gov. Campaign sites, social media, and news articles are not sources for the feeds.

Methodology

Federal actions come from official records. Executive orders, rules, and proposed rules come from the Federal Register API. House roll calls come from the Clerk's XML files. Senate roll calls come from the Senate LIS XML files. Congress.gov bills are included only when an api.data.gov key is configured for the scraper; until then the status page says they were skipped. Each record links to the official page or XML.

House periodic transaction reports come from the Clerk's annual filing index. Electronic reports are read from the PDF text. Scanned PDFs are listed with a link and are not transcribed. Senate filings are not downloaded. Each senator row links to the Senate eFD search, which asks the reader to accept the site's terms. That search is not automated.

A run keeps a cursor per source and continues after an interruption. Running the same dates again replaces records that share an id, so it does not add duplicates. The federal window already saved is 15 October 2026 through 16 November 2026. House trade rows in the feed are filings from 1 November 2026 through 16 November 2026. Later runs continue from the saved cursor unless a date range is requested.

AI and data disclaimer

Pages display the fields in the JSON feeds. Any summary added later may be drafted with automated tools and can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. The official filing or government page is the record. This site is not legal, financial, or voting advice, and it is not affiliated with the U.S. government.

A periodic transaction report is a disclosure. It is not evidence of improper conduct. A missing row means the scrapers have not collected it here, not that the record does not exist.
