About

Nonpartisan

does not support or oppose candidates, parties, or legislation. It publishes public records and points at the original documents. Party labels, when a filing or official source includes them, are shown as reported.

Official sources only

Records are meant to come only from official publications. Planned sources are Congress.gov, House and Senate periodic transaction reports filed under the STOCK Act, the Federal Register, and executive-order publications on whitehouse.gov. Campaign sites, social media, and news articles are not sources for the feeds.

AI and data disclaimer

Pages display the fields in the JSON feeds. Any summary added later may be drafted with automated tools and can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. The official filing or government page is the record. This site is not legal, financial, or voting advice, and it is not affiliated with the U.S. government.

A periodic transaction report is a disclosure. It is not evidence of improper conduct. A missing row means the scrapers have not collected it here, not that the record does not exist.
